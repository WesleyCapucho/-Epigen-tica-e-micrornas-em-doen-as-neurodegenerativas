The author chose **Review** on 14 October (the cover letter says "for consideration as a Review"), from the types the page lists (Original Research, Review, Brief Report and Comment, among others). The summaries do not say which type a systematic review with meta-analysis belongs to, so the choice rests on the author's judgement and on the instruction, as read in the summaries, that a Review states who had the idea, who did the search and analysis, and who drafted and revised; the Author contributions statement does. Confirm the type and its limits on the submission page. The body text is about 6,100 words (Introduction to Conclusions, captions excluded) with 74 references.
- A keyword rule. The manuscript gives six keywords, which fits the 4 to 6 rule that the sister journal *Cellular and Molecular Neurobiology* states.
- Any line-numbering requirement. The file is line-numbered, which does no harm.
- A journal rule on reporting guidelines (PRISMA-DTA is followed) or on registration (the review was registered retrospectively on OSF).
- A graphical abstract, highlights, or a specific figure-width rule beyond 84/174 mm.

## External review of the manuscript (14 October 2026)

A reviewer read the manuscript against the journal's instruction page and returned a revised copy and a report. Each change and each flagged item was checked against the repository data before it was accepted, and `scripts/35_manuscript_numbers.py` now computes the counts those checks needed (`flow.acc_*`, `flow.abs_only`, `flow.no_se_*`, `flow.recall_unassessed`, `des.assessable`, `n.auc_round90`, `prior.*.studies`, `rob.alt_*`). What changed:

- **Wording that the data or the registration did not support.** "Pre-specified" and "fixed in advance" were removed for the selection rule, the subgroups and the interval-plausibility rule, because the registration is retrospective (the same wording was removed from the README, the Methods, the data dictionary and the PRISMA-DTA checklist). Statements about design features divide by the 35 pooled studies whose design could be assessed, not by 37. "Above 0.90" replaces "0.90 or more" (four AUCs are above 0.90; two more, 0.898 and 0.895, are labelled 0.90 in Fig. 4 after rounding). The evidence category "comparison with paired brain tissue" is now named in the Methods and in the Fig. 6 caption.
- **Format, as read from the journal page by the reviewer** (the page itself could not be opened from here, so these items rest on that reading and on search summaries): references in Springer Basic style (no issue number, "et al" without a comma, MDPI article numbers taken from the DOI), figure captions without final punctuation, no line numbering, the page number as the only field, labels in plain type, descriptive alternative text for the figures, and the supplement named Online Resource 1.
- **What the check found in the data.** Of the 129 primary studies whose abstract states an accuracy measure, 95 are from PubMed; 55 of those have an open full text and 40 do not, and none of the 40 was extracted. 45 of the 129 contributed values (16 from the abstract alone), and 44 contributed none, with no reason logged record by record. The manuscript now says so in the Results and the limitations. This is a selection risk: several of the 40 look, from their titles, like eligible studies of circulating miRNAs. Obtaining those full texts and extracting them would be the most useful way to strengthen the review and would require a new version of the archive.
- **Kept as it was.** The heading "Declaration of Generative AI in Scientific Writing" is the one used by the published Research article of the journal supplied as a model; the reviewer suggested a different heading that could not be confirmed on the journal page. Chen P 2026 is not a data error: the abstract gives sensitivity, specificity and group sizes but no AUC, so the study is outside the AUC pool and inside the sensitivity-specificity analysis.
